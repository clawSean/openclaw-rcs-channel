# Changelog

## 2026.10.15

- Reframed the package around its intended business users, professional use
  cases, and operational value.
- Synchronized the final reviewed safety and runtime-shape changes from the
  preserved upstream implementation.
- Added explicit proof boundaries for current OpenClaw compatibility versus the
  still-unproven paid carrier/device round trip.
- Added durable provenance, credit, and citation guidance for future official or
  bundled RCS work.
- Refreshed ClawHub catalog copy and discovery topics. No new carrier or sender
  certification claim is made by this release.

## 2026.8.1

- Initial community ClawHub release derived from
  `openclaw/openclaw#105025`.
- Added RCS-only text/media delivery, signed and durable inbound callbacks,
  pairing and allowlists, multi-account support, lifecycle state, and
  delivery/read observations.
